# Pair a device with all your environments

When one device already reaches your environments, it can pair another device with all of them
at once.

## Pair another device

1. On a device that is connected to your environments, open **Settings → Environments** and
   choose **Pair a device** (on iPhone or iPad, **Pair another device**).
2. On the new device, open **Add environment** and scan the code, or paste the link.

The code is single use and expires after five minutes. Anyone who has it can use every environment
it lists, so share it only with your own devices.

With many environments, the code is split into several smaller codes so each stays easy to scan.
Use the arrows to show each one, and on the new device tap the scanner once for each code. It
tells you which code to scan next. **Copy link** always copies one link with every environment,
for pasting on a computer.

Environments the new device already reaches are left as they are. An environment it has saved but
can no longer reach, for example because its pairing expired, is paired again.

## Pairings renew themselves

A paired device renews its access to each environment while it is in use, so a device you open at
least every two weeks stays paired. A device left unused for longer than 30 days needs pairing
again.

After a renewal, a host's client list shows the device twice until its older access expires, at
most two weeks later. To remove a device from a host, revoke both entries.
